WhatsApp

Getting your WhatsApp
chat ready.

WhatsApp has a built-in export — no extra tools needed. Export straight from the app, then drop the file below. Takes about a minute.

1
Open the group chat

Open WhatsApp on your phone and go to the group chat you want to print.

2
Export the chat

Tap the group name at the top to open group info. Scroll down and tap Export Chat. When prompted, choose With Media to include photos and videos.

On iPhone

Tap the group name → scroll to bottom → Export Chat → With Media → save to Files or share to yourself.

On Android

Tap the group name → More → Export Chat → With Media → send to yourself via email or save to drive.

3
Upload your zip and order

Transfer the zip to your computer — AirDrop, email yourself, or save to iCloud/Google Drive. Then drop it below and we'll carry it straight into the wizard.
